## Lifecycle

`not_started → draft → in_review → approved → deprecated`

A casual prompt does not move a record to approved. Approval records the actor.

## What to capture

Capture name, sector, offer, audience, and mascot yes/no. Prefer the interview over filling this whole brief at once.
